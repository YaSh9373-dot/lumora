**Project name:** Lumora: AI Content Creator Marketplace

**Description (~120 words):**
Lumora is an AI-native marketplace connecting brands with AI filmmakers, animators and designers. Creator profiles go beyond a portfolio: each lists the tools and models used, with proficiency and verification, plus skills, formats and structured commercial terms, and every piece can show *how it was made* (workflow steps, prompt example, generations, hours). Brands search with accurate combined filters, browse a Works masonry, and write structured briefs covering deliverables, aspect ratios, durations, style, platforms, budget and full commercial-use rights. An AI Brief Builder (Gemini, with a deterministic fallback) turns one rough line into a complete brief. Explainable matching ranks creators with reasons, a computed trust score surfaces verification signals, and engagements run from invite to delivery through a validated pipeline.
